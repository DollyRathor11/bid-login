Acxxel GeM Checking...
Disqualified & Clarification Bid Tracking
  • Scan Disqualified Bid – saves bids where Acxxel was disqualified, with the reason, to Disqualified Bids.
  • Scan Clarification – saves bids where the buyer asked for a clarification to Clarification Bid.

Bid To Be Participated
  • Scan Bid To Be Participated – reads new bids from Ongoing Bids and saves those that pass all bid rules to Bid To Be Participated.
  • My Representation – needs the GeM login with User Id "Representation". Opens each bid's My Representations and saves the ones with entries to Post Bid > My Representation. It only reads; nothing is submitted.

Awarded Bid/RA Ranking
  • Scan All Awarded Bid/RA Results – saves every seller's rank and price (L1, L2…) of awarded bids to Financial Rankings.

Counter Offer (Match Price)
  • Scan Counter Offer – saves pending counter offers (your price vs L1 price) to Post Bid > Match Price.

Product Scan
  • Scan Products – saves Acxxel's GeM catalogue products (model, price, specs, image) to the product directory and an Excel per category.
